# QR Reader — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

QR Reader is a Chrome extension that decodes a QR code in an area of a web page you select.

## What the extension accesses

When you click the QR Reader toolbar button (or use its keyboard shortcut), the extension:

- shows a selection overlay on the current tab, and
- takes a screenshot of the visible part of that tab, so it can read the QR code inside the rectangle you draw.

The extension does nothing on any page until you click it.

## What happens to that data

- The screenshot is cropped and decoded **locally in your browser**, then discarded. It is never saved or sent anywhere.
- The decoded value is shown only to you. It is copied to your clipboard only when you click **Copy**.
- The extension makes **no network requests**. It has no servers, analytics, tracking, advertising or accounts.
- Nothing is stored on your device by the extension.

## Sharing

No data is collected, so no data is sold, shared with or transferred to anyone.
